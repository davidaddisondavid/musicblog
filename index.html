Front Page

Welcome to the beginning of a website.
I don't know what this will end up being, but for now there's a blog and a weekly-updated list of gigs happening in London.

I named the website after the EP Howl or Buzz Under The Influence of Heat by the Minutemen, honestly because I was looking through what I'd listened to recently, and thought that it made for a cool name. But, if I was lying, I'd say that it's because I want this website to be like a punk zine, something made out of love and obsession for music, people and places – held together with all the html knowledge I've scraped together in the last few weeks.
I hope that this site can be your life.
